Our story

A school that started with one classroom and a garden.

Little Sparks opened in 2011 with 14 children and a single sunny room. Fifteen years on, the garden's bigger, the classrooms are full, and the promise hasn't changed: every child learns best when they feel safe, seen, and free to be curious.

Our approach

We follow a play-based curriculum built around three ideas: children learn through doing, through stories, and through each other. Structured lessons are short; open-ended play, building, and outdoor exploration take up the rest of the day.

Every teacher is trained in early-childhood education, and every classroom has two adults, so no child ever goes unnoticed.

  • 1
    Kindness first. Conflict is a chance to learn, not a reason to punish.
  • 2
    Curiosity over correctness. A good question matters more than a right answer.
  • 3
    Real childhood. Mud, noise, and mess are part of learning, not a mess to avoid.
  • 4
    Partnership with parents. You know your child best — we build on that.

Joining Little Sparks is simpler than you'd think.

Four steps, most families are done within two weeks. No exams for anyone under 6 — just a friendly visit and a conversation.

1

Book a visit

Walk through a real classroom, meet the teachers, and see a normal school day in action.

2

Submit the form

A short form with your child's age, any allergies, and your preferred program.

3

Meet-and-greet

A relaxed 20-minute chat between your child, a teacher, and you — no tests involved.

4

Welcome aboard

Confirm the seat, pick a start date, and we'll send over a settling-in guide.

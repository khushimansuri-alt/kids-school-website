Why families choose us

A few things that make a Little Sparks day different from an ordinary school day.

🎨

Play-first learning

Half the day is guided play — because that's how young children actually learn best.

🌱

Outdoor time, daily

Rain or shine, every class spends real time outside in our garden and sandpit.

👩‍🏫

Small class sizes

No more than 16 children per class, with two teachers who know every name.

🍎

Fresh, home-style meals

Snacks and lunch cooked on-site every morning, with allergy-aware menus.

Programs by age group

Each stage has its own pace, room, and rhythm.

Ages 2–3

Playgroup

Gentle first steps away from home — songs, sensory play, and lots of cuddly reassurance.

Ages 4–5

Nursery

Letters, numbers, and friendships start here, through stories and hands-on projects.

Ages 6–8

Primary Steps

Reading, writing, and early science — building real confidence, one question at a time.
